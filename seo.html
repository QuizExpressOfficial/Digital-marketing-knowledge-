SEO Kya Hai? (Introduction)

SEO ka pura naam Search Engine Optimization hai. Yeh ek aisi organic technique hai jiske zariye website ko Google par bina ad diye top par laya jata hai.

घरेलू उदाहरण (Real Life Example):

Maan lijiye aapne apne ghar ke andar ek dukaan kholi. Ab dukaan ke andar saman kaise sajaya hai (On-Page), baahar board kaisa lagaya hai taaki log raste se aate waqt recommend karein (Off-Page), aur dukaan ka rasta kitna saaf aur aasan hai (Technical) — yeh sab milkar tay karte hain ki dukaan kitni chalegi!

SEO Three Pillars

1. On-Page 2. Off-Page 3. Technical

1. On-Page SEO (Detailed)

Isme hum apni website ke andar mojood content aur code ko Google ke anusaar behtar banate hain taaki search engine ko saaf-saaf samajh aaye ki page kis baare mein hai.

  • Title Tag & Meta Description: Google par jo neela title aur chhota paragraph dikhta hai, usme main keyword ka hona zaroori hai.
  • Heading Structure (H1, H2, H3): Page par ek hi main H1 heading honi chahiye, aur baaki subheadings ke liye H2, H3 ka kramic use hona chahiye.
  • Keyword Density & Placement: Article mein keyword ko bina spam kiye (natural tarike se) shuruvaati 100 shabdo mein use karna.
  • Image Alt Text: Har photo ke andar alt tag lagana taaki Google ki blind crawler machine padh sake ki tasveer kis cheez ki hai.

घरेलू उदाहरण:

Jaise aap ghar me rasoi ka samaan sahi dabbe par 'Chini', 'Namak' ka label lagakar saza kar rakhte hain, wahi On-Page SEO hai taaki koi bhi anjaan aakar turant samajh jaye.

2. Off-Page SEO (Detailed)

Yeh website ke baahar ki jati hai jiska mukhya maksad internet par apni website ki authority aur trust badhana hota hai.

  • Backlinks (Link Building): Jab koi doosri badi aur vishwasniya website aapki website ka link apne page par deti hai, toh Google ki nazar mein aapki value badh jati hai.
  • Guest Blogging: Kisi doosre ke blog par jaakar apne field ka article likhna aur wahan se apni site ka link lena.
  • Social Media Signals: Facebook, Instagram ya Twitter par apne content ko share karna aur wahan se audience ko website par lana.
  • Brand Mentions: Internet par aapke brand ya website ki charcha hona, chahe link jude ya na jude.

घरेलू उदाहरण:

Mohalle mein agar aapko sabhi log achha aur imaandar bolte hain (doosro ki gawahi), toh naya vyakti bhi aap par turant bharosa kar leta hai. Yehhi Off-Page SEO (Backlinks) hai!

3. Technical SEO (Detailed)

Iska matlab website ke backend aur infrastructure ko theek karna hota hai taaki search engine ke bots bina kisi rukawat ke site ko crawl aur index kar sakein.

  • Site Speed & Core Web Vitals: Website kitni tezi se khul rahi hai aur mobile par user experience kaisa hai.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Website ka har ek page mobile, tablet aur computer par alag-alag screen size ke hisaab se perfectly fit hona chahiye.
  • SSL Certificate (HTTPS): Website secure hai ya nahi (lock icon), yeh aajkal ranking ka sabse bada factor hai.
  • XML Sitemap & Robots.txt: Google ko batana ki website par kaunse page crawl karne hain aur kaunse chhupane hain.

घरेलू उदाहरण:

Jaise ghar ka bijli connection, paani ki pipeline aur mazboot darwaaze andar se theek honge tabhi aap chain se reh sakenge. Technical SEO wahi majboot buniyaad hai!
