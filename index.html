Digital Marketing Guide

1. Digital Marketing Kya Hai?

Digital marketing ka matlab hota hai internet (jaise mobile, laptop, social media, Google) ka use karke apne kisi samaan, service ya business ka prachar (promotion) karna.

2. Digital Marketing Kyon Zaroori Hai?

Aaj ke samay mein sabhi log apna zyada se zyada waqt mobile aur internet par spend karte hain. Isliye agar aapka business online nahi hai, toh aap apne bohot bade customer base ko miss kar rahe hain.

3. Real Life Example

Maan lijiye aapka ek kapdo ka local store hai. Agar aap sirf dukan khol kar baithenge toh wahi log aayenge jo aas-paas rehte hain. Lekin agar aap Instagram ya Facebook par apne kapdo ki reels aur ads chalate hain, toh aapke shehar ke hazaron log aapki dukan ke baare mein jaan jayenge aur online ya offline aakar kharidi karenge. Isse kehte hain digital marketing ki taakat!
