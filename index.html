What is Digital Marketing Hub?
Internet aur online technologies ka use karke apne business ya brand ko grow karne ka sabse behtareen platform.

Digital Marketing Kya Hai?

Digital Marketing ka matlab hota hai internet (jaise mobile, laptop, YouTube, Facebook, Google) ka use karke apne kisi samaan ya business ka prachar (promotion) karna.

Yeh kyun zaroori hai? Aajkal har koi apna jyadatar samay phone aur internet par hi bitata hai. Isliye agar aapko apni dukaan ya cheezon ke baare mein logon ko batana hai, toh aapko wahan jaana hoga jahan log sabse jyada hain— yaani ki internet par!

Ek Asaan Example:

Maan lijiye aapne naye sports shoes banaye. Agar aap sadak par poster lagayenge toh wahan se sirf wahi log dekh payenge jo us raste se gujrenge. Lekin agar aap internet par digital marketing karenge, toh aapke shoes ka ad hazaron-lakhon logon ke mobile par ek hi baar mein dikh jayega jo shaukeen hain!
